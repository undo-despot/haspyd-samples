# found ◆ знайдені звуки

An open collection of **found sounds**. Not all of them are mine: this is a
collection, not a claim of authorship. I do not own, sell or license the sounds
I did not make. Known authors and sources are listed in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

Відкрита колекція **знайдених звуків**. Тут не тільки мої звуки: це колекція,
а не заява про авторство. Де я знаю автора й джерело — вони в [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

## Is this your sound? / Це твій звук?

If you made something here, you can:

- **claim it** — tell me, and I will add your name, link and license;
- **correct it** — if the credit or license is wrong;
- **remove it** — ask, and I will delete it, no questions asked.

Open an [Issue](https://github.com/undo-despot/haspyd-samples/issues) with the file name.

Якщо ти автор якогось звуку — напиши в Issues: додам твоє ім'я, виправлю
підпис або видалю файл, без питань.

## Using the sounds / Використання

Each sound keeps the license of its original author (see SOURCES.md).
A sound with no license listed has unknown status: fine for learning and
playing around, check with the author before releasing something with it.

## У Strudel

```js
samples('github:undo-despot/haspyd-samples')
s("ae_steel:12 ae_kick ae_sh09:1 fs_fx")
```
